How it works

  1. Your attention signal is read continuously while you work.
  2. It is translated into a focus level from 0 to 100%.
  3. Changes in that level trigger gentle suggestions, such as a break.

This preview is interactive but simulated: drag the slider to see how each level is labeled. It does not access your camera.
